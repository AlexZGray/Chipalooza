\end{tikzpicture}

\large Accuracy $= -0.107\,\mathrm{LSB}$ ($-4.5\,\mathrm{mV}$) @ $D=62$ \qquad INL $\approx 0$ \qquad DNL $\approx 0$ \qquad Monotonic (min.\ step $42.19\,\mathrm{mV}$)
\end{center}

\large The constant $-0.107\,\mathrm{LSB}$ error is the MCP6001 macromodel's built-in $4.5\,\mathrm{mV}$ offset. INL and DNL are zero because all resistors are identical.
